Questions before you send the deck.

How is Tellar different from DocSend + Loom + ChatGPT?

Those are three tools that don't know about each other. DocSend logs views but can't talk. Loom has narration but no analytics. ChatGPT can answer but doesn't know your deck. Tellar is the three, on the same object: every slide is a document, a video, and a conversation — and every event flows into the same analytics.

Can my agent hallucinate or leak data it shouldn't?

No — and we take that seriously. The agent is grounded on your slides, your narration transcripts, and the KB you attach. If the answer isn't there, it refuses politely and offers to ping you. Every answer shows the exact slide or page it came from.

What happens to a shared link if I realize I made a mistake?

Revoke it. From the dashboard or viewer settings, any link can be expired, passphrase-protected, or replaced — retroactively. Viewers who had it open mid-session get bumped on the next slide.

Do you support SSO, SCIM, and compliance docs?

Yes — on the Scale plan. SOC 2 Type II in progress (Q3 2026). Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace OIDC. DPA and custom MSA on request.

Can viewers download the deck as a PDF?

Only if you turn it on. By default, no: shared tellars play in the browser with the watermark stamped across every slide. You can enable PDF export per share.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

14 days, no card. If you love it, we ask for payment on day 15. If you don't, your tellars stay — they just drop to Hobby limits.
