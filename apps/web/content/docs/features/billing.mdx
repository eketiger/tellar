---
title: Billing
description: Stripe Checkout, Portal, and webhook pipeline.
---

Plans:

- **Free** — 1 workspace, 1 teller, 1,000 agent queries / month
- **Pro** — $49/mo — unlimited tellers, 5 seats
- **Scale** — $149/mo — 25 seats, priority support
- **Enterprise** — custom

Users upgrade via Stripe Checkout (`POST /api/billing/create-checkout-session`)
and manage their card / invoices via the Customer Portal
(`POST /api/billing/create-portal-session`).

The webhook at `POST /api/billing/webhook` verifies Stripe's signature, then upserts the
`Subscription` and `Invoice` rows for every lifecycle event.
