---
title: Tellers
description: What a tellar is and how it is stored.
---

A **tellar** is a sequence of slides with optional narration recordings and an attached
knowledge base. Everything lives in Postgres via Prisma. Slides have an index, an eyebrow,
a title (which can contain HTML for inline emphasis), a subtitle, and speaker notes.

Tellars are versioned through a `revision` counter that increments on every meaningful edit.
