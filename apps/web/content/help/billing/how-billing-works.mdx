---
title: How billing works
description: One sentence per thing you need to know.
category: billing
---

- Every workspace has a plan. Paid plans are billed monthly via Stripe.
- Charges run on your card automatically on your renewal date.
- You can cancel any time — you keep access until the end of the current period.
- Card and billing address are managed in the Stripe-hosted Customer Portal; we never see them.
