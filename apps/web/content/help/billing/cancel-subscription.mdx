---
title: Cancel your subscription
description: Stay paid until the end of the period, then drop to Free.
category: billing
---

1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Manage billing** to open the Stripe Customer Portal.
3. Cancel the subscription. Your plan stays active until `currentPeriodEnd`, after which the
   workspace drops to Free.

Your tellars, data, and team members are preserved — only the paid features get gated.
