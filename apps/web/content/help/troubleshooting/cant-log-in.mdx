---
title: I can't log in
description: Provider conflicts, wrong password, cookies.
category: troubleshooting
---

Most login failures come from one of three things:

- **Wrong password** — reset it via the sign-in screen.
- **Provider conflict** — the email exists under a different OAuth provider. Use the provider
  you signed up with.
- **Blocked cookies** — Tellar uses an httpOnly session cookie. Unblock cookies for the domain.
