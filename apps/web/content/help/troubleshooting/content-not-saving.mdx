---
title: Content isn't saving
description: When a slide edit doesn't stick.
category: troubleshooting
---

The editor autosaves on blur. If you see the title revert after typing, check:

- Network tab — `PATCH /api/slides/{id}` should return 200.
- Session — if your JWT expired (every 7 days) the save will 401. Refresh and log in again.
- Role — Viewers can't edit. Ask an admin to promote you.
