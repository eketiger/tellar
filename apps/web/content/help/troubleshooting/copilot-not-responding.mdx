---
title: Copilot isn't responding
description: Common causes and the fix.
category: troubleshooting
---

- **Daily limit reached** — you get 50 Copilot calls a day. Upgrade or wait until tomorrow.
- **Model temporarily throttled** — Anthropic sometimes 429s us. Try again in a few seconds.
- **No API key configured** — if the deployment has no `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, Copilot falls back
  to a deterministic mock. Ask your admin.
