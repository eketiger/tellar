---
title: Create your first tellar
description: From signup to a shareable link in five minutes.
category: getting-started
---

1. Sign up at [/register](/register).
2. On the dashboard, click **+ New tellar** and give it a title.
3. Edit slides inline. Click **Rewrite** for a Claude-powered suggestion.
4. Open the **Share** tab, pick Email gate, and add your investor domains.
5. Copy the link. You're done.
