---
title: Invite team members
description: Add editors and viewers to your workspace.
category: getting-started
---

Open **Settings → Members**. Enter an email and pick a role:

- **Admin** — everything except transferring workspace ownership.
- **Editor** — create and edit tellars.
- **Viewer** — read-only access to internal tellars.

They get an invite email and, once they accept, show up as **active**. You can remove them
at any time from the same screen.
